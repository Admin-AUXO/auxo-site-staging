Cookies & Local Storage

Cookie Policy

How AUXO Data Labs uses cookies, local storage, consent controls, and analytics-related technologies on auxodata.com.

How consent, analytics, and browser-side storage work on auxodata.com.

  • Consent-first analytics
  • Local storage transparency
  • No active ad cookies today

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Current state. AUXO uses consent-aware analytics and browser storage for preferences. At the time of this update, we do not knowingly activate advertising cookies on the public site.

1. What This Policy Covers

This Cookie Policy explains how Rapple International FZE, trading as AUXO Data Labs, uses cookies and similar technologies on auxodata.com.

It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

2. What We Mean by Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use first-party cookies to run the site and, where similar browser-side storage is involved, we refer to these technologies together where that is the clearest explanation.

3. How Consent Works on This Site

We use Google Tag Manager to manage Google Analytics (GA4). With Google Consent Mode, analytics and advertising storage are denied by default: GA4 sets no analytics cookies until you accept through the cookie banner. Essential functionality needed for security or core preferences can still operate without consent.

  • Accept: records your consent and loads analytics.
  • Reject: records your choice and keeps analytics off.

You can reopen these controls through the Cookie Settings button in the footer.

4. Categories We Use

4.1 Strictly Necessary

These support core site operation, consent state, and security-related behaviour. Without them, parts of the experience may not work as expected.

4.2 Preferences

These remember choices you make on the site, such as your saved consent selection, so the site behaves consistently on your next visit.

4.3 Analytics

These help us understand aggregate site usage and page engagement. They are optional and only intended to operate when you grant the relevant consent.

4.4 Marketing

We keep a marketing category in the consent interface for future expansion, but we do not knowingly activate marketing or advertising cookies on the public site as of June 10, 2026.

5. Current Cookie and Storage Declaration

Identifier Provider Storage Type Purpose Typical Duration Category

This table reflects the tools we can verify from the current site code. If we materially change cookie usage, we will update this page and the consent flow.

6. Managing Cookies and Browser Storage

You can manage non-essential categories through our consent interface, and you can also clear cookies or local storage directly through your browser. Blocking storage entirely may affect features such as saved preferences or consent persistence.

  • Google Chrome
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Mozilla Firefox

7. Third-Party Tools and Cross-Border Processing

Some site functions rely on third-party services such as Google Analytics (loaded only after consent) or Google Calendar booking flows. Those services may process technical request data outside your country.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may change this policy when the site, our tooling, or legal requirements change. The last-updated date at the top of the page shows the current version.
